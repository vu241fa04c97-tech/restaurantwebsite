Signature Chicken Biryani

₹390

Our signature masterpiece: Slow-cooked dum biryani layered with 2-year aged dehradun basmati rice, tender farm-reared chicken steeped in saffron yogurt marinade, caramelized shallots, fresh garden mint, and secret royal spice blends. Served in a sealed copper handi with chilled burani raita and mirchi ka salan.

◆ Saffron Infused
◆ Dum Slow Cooked
◆ Royal Clay & Copper
◆ Burani Raita Included

Royal Paneer Tikka

₹340

Prime malai cottage cheese cubes marinated for 18 hours in artisanal hung curd, Kashmiri deghi mirch, carom seeds, ginger-garlic paste, and toasted gram flour. Skewered with crunchy bell peppers and charred over hardwood charcoal embers inside our clay tandoor oven.

◆ 18-Hour Marinade
◆ Fresh Malai Paneer
◆ Charcoal Charred
◆ Mint Laccha Chutney
